About medicube

Korean Skincare,
Backed by Data

medicube is a Korean skincare brand by APR Corporation — the team behind some of the best-reviewed K-beauty devices and dermocosmetics on the market. Founded in Seoul in 2016, medicube set out to bring clinic-grade treatments home: not watered-down versions, but the real thing adapted for daily use.

The skincare range covers everything from the viral Zero Pore Pads (BHA + AHA toner pads that clear pores and tackle acne) to the Collagen Night Wrapping Mask, Collagen Jelly Cream, and the cult-favourite Super Cica Daily Quick Mask — a 60-second face mask used by millions across 30+ countries. Every product goes through independent dermatologist testing before launch.

The AGE-R device line — starting with the Booster Pro — was built to solve a real problem: even the best serums only penetrate so far. Electroporation fixes that, opening temporary micro-channels so ingredients absorb up to 500% more effectively. It's why medicube reviews consistently highlight visible results within weeks, and why the brand has ranked #1 in Korea's beauty device category three years running.

Advanced Technology

6 Technologies, 1 Device

The medicube AGE-R Booster Pro combines six clinical technologies in one device. Each mode targets a specific skin concern.

  • Booster Mode

    01

    Booster Mode

    Electroporation opens temporary micro-channels in skin. Serum absorption increases by up to 500% compared to topical application alone.

  • Air Shot

    02

    Air Shot

    Creates tiny pathways on the skin surface so ingredients absorb more effectively. No irritation, no downtime.

  • MC Mode

    03

    MC Mode

    Targets sagging around the eyes and mouth with microcurrent stimulation. Firms areas that lose volume with age.

  • Derma Shot

    04

    Derma Shot

    Focuses on areas that need more definition - jawline, cheekbones, and nasolabial folds. Uses targeted electrical pulses.

  • Sonic Vibration

    05

    Sonic Vibration

    Gentle vibration improves product absorption and skin texture. Works as a finishing step after serum application.

  • LED Therapy

    06

    LED Therapy

    Red and near-infrared LED wavelengths stimulate collagen production. Built-in skin contact sensor activates light only when device touches skin.

About medicube

The AGE-R Booster Pro is one of the best-selling skincare devices in Korea. In clinical tests, users reported visible improvements in skin texture and glow within 2–4 weeks of daily 5-minute sessions.

Apply a water-based gel serum to clean skin, then glide the device over your face following the AGE-R app guided routine. Each session takes about 5 minutes. The app tracks your modes and intensity automatically.

medicube products are not tested on animals. Check individual product packaging for specific cruelty-free certifications.

Shop directly on the medicube Official Website, or find medicube products on Amazon, YesStyle, Olive Young, and authorized retailers worldwide.

The medicube Zero Pore Pads 2.0 contain BHA and AHA to clear pores and reduce blackheads with regular use. The Booster Pro's Air Shot mode also helps with pore care by improving product penetration.

Apply a small amount to clean skin as the last step in your routine. The Collagen Jelly Cream works as a moisturiser and occlusive — it seals in the layers underneath while delivering hydrolysed collagen and peptides. Use morning or night, or both. It pairs well with the AGE-R Booster Gel Serum when using the Booster Pro device.

The medicube Zero Pore Pads 2.0 are BHA and AHA toner pads designed to clear congested pores, reduce blackheads, and smooth skin texture. Swipe one pad across cleansed skin 2–3 times a week. They are one of the most-reviewed medicube products globally and a staple in K-beauty skincare routines.

Most medicube skincare products are dermatologist-tested and formulated for sensitive and acne-prone skin. The Zero Pore Pads use BHA (salicylic acid) which is specifically effective for acne and blackheads. The Super Cica Daily Quick Mask uses centella asiatica to calm inflammation.
